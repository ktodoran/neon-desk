# Shipping & Returns Policy

## Shipping Information

### Domestic Shipping (United States)

**Standard Shipping**

- Delivery time: 5-7 business days
- Cost: Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- Carrier: USPS or FedEx Ground

**Express Shipping**

- Delivery time: 2-3 business days
- Cost: $12.99
- Carrier: FedEx 2Day

**Overnight Shipping**

- Delivery time: Next business day (order by 2 PM EST)
- Cost: $24.99
- Carrier: FedEx Overnight

### International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on:

- Destination country
- Package weight and dimensions
- Selected shipping method

**Estimated delivery times:**

- Canada & Mexico: 7-14 business days
- Europe: 10-21 business days
- Asia Pacific: 14-28 business days
- Other regions: 14-30 business days

**Customs & Duties:** International customers are responsible for any customs fees or import duties. These are not included in the shipping cost.

### Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. During peak seasons (holidays, sales), processing may take 3-5 business days.

You'll receive an email confirmation when your order is placed and another when it ships with tracking information.

## Returns & Exchanges

### Return Policy

We offer a **30-day return policy** from the date of delivery. Items must be:

- Unused and in original condition
- In original packaging with tags attached
- Accompanied by proof of purchase

### How to Return

1. **Contact us first:** Email support@example.com or use our return portal
2. **Receive return authorization:** We'll provide a return authorization number (RMA)
3. **Package your return:** Include the RMA number and original receipt
4. **Ship it back:** Use the prepaid return label we provide (domestic returns only)

### Return Shipping

- **Domestic returns:** Free prepaid return label provided
- **International returns:** Customer is responsible for return shipping costs
- **Damaged/defective items:** We cover return shipping costs

### Refunds

Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days after we receive and inspect your return. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

**Note:** Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

### Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item:

1. Return the original item following our return process
2. Place a new order for the desired item

### Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

- Items marked as "Final Sale"
- Personalized or customized products
- Items damaged by customer misuse
- Items returned after 30 days

### Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

- Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
- Provide photos of the damage/defect
- We'll arrange for immediate replacement or full refund, including return shipping

## Questions?

If you have questions about shipping or returns, please contact our customer service team at support@example.com or use the live chat feature on our website.
